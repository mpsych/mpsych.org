Ana Maria Fahlböck is a Master's student in Medical Informatics at TU Wien, currently a visiting researcher at the University of Massachusetts Boston. She works on visualization and human-centered AI in healthcare, designing interactive tools that make complex information easier to explore and support learning in clinical settings.

She brings a data feminist perspective to her research, asking how data is collected, represented, and communicated, and who gets left out along the way.

Her research stay is supported by a Marshall Plan Scholarship.
